About Me

Full-Stack AI Engineer and Digital Operator

I'm an e-commerce and digital marketing professional with hands-on experience building and scaling online businesses from the ground up. Whether managing international paid media at a national brand or architecting a Shopify storefront with custom auction workflows, I bring both strategic thinking and ground-level execution to everything I work on.

My background covers paid media, email marketing, SEO, marketplace operations, and full-cycle e-commerce management. I've worked across Meta, Google, Klaviyo, Shopify, and eBay, and I know how to connect those tools into a system that actually converts.

I am a Full-Stack AI Engineer. Since 2022 I have built a strong foundation in reading, editing, and applying code, and I use tools like Claude and ChatGPT to extend that foundation into more advanced builds and designs. AI does not replace my technical judgment; it amplifies it. The result is the ability to research, prototype, and ship work that would typically require a full team.

Outside of client and contract work, I run my own eBay reselling operation under the Wayward Traderz brand and create content for Wayward Third, a personal brand active across the web, Twitter/X, and social media.
