# Name candidates

Twelve-plus candidates, each with one line of reasoning, the likely domain,
and any collision found by searching (checked 2026-08-24). Collisions were
found by web search only; **availability must be confirmed at a registrar by
the owner. Nothing here reserves anything.** The bar throughout: a name that
survives being said aloud once and typed from memory.

## Keep or replace "Bearings"?

**Keep it.** The honest case against: it is a real word with heavy existing
use, the .com belongs to ball bearings forever, and small compass apps
already squat the app-store search results. The case for: none of those
collisions is a consumer map-learning product, the word is the product's
promise in one breath ("get your bearings"), it survives being said aloud
once and typed from memory, and every alternative above either collides
worse (Crowsflight, Wayfair, Daytrip), or trades recognition for
availability (Two Towns Over, Townline). A modifier domain
(**bearings.guide** or **getbearings.app**) resolves the .com problem the
way most modern products do. If a truly clean name is wanted instead, **Two
Towns Over** is the strongest replacement on the list: zero collisions
found and it cannot be misheard.

Note on TLD renewals (see COSTS.md): .guide and .app renew higher than .com
(commonly $20-40/yr); confirm renewal price, not just first-year price, at
the registrar.
